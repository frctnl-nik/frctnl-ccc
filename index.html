How this is calculated, and where the benchmarks come from

Debtor days (DSO) = trade debtors ÷ annual revenue × 365.
Stock days (DIO) = stock ÷ annual cost of sales × 365.
Creditor days (DPO) = trade creditors ÷ annual cost of sales × 365.
Unbilled days (optional) = unbilled work ÷ annual revenue × 365.
Cash conversion cycle = stock days + unbilled days + debtor days − creditor days.

Cash tied up = trade debtors + stock + unbilled work − trade creditors. Potential release at typical levels adds up the cash you would free by moving each measure that is worse than the middle of the typical range for your business type to that middle point.

Year-end balances can differ from the average through the year, especially for seasonal businesses. Revenue is taken ex VAT, so debtor days may read a little lower than your credit control reports if those include VAT.

Debtor-day ranges are informed by payment-time data for UK sectors published by Novuna Business Cash Flow (published May 2025, updated March 2026). Stock-day, creditor-day and unbilled-day ranges are Frctnl Finance rules of thumb, not published statistics. Treat every range as a starting point for a conversation.
